Política de privacidad

The Special One no recoge, almacena ni comparte datos personales. No hay cuentas, anuncios, analíticas ni conexiones a servidores. La partida y los ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo y puedes borrarlos desde Ajustes → Borrar datos o desinstalando la app. La app no solicita permisos especiales.

Privacy policy

The Special One does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or server connections. Your saved game and settings are stored only on your device; you can erase them from Settings → Erase data or by uninstalling the app. The app requests no special permissions.
